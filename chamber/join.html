NP Membership

Benefits include:

  • Listing in the chamber business directory
  • Access to chamber networking events
  • Monthly chamber newsletter

COST: Free

Bronze Membership

Benefits include:

  • Everything in NP Membership
  • Discounted rates on chamber events
  • Two free training workshops each year

COST: N25,000 annual

Silver Membership

Benefits include:

  • Everything in Bronze Membership
  • Spotlight position on the chamber home page
  • Free entry to all chamber training events
  • Advertising space in the monthly newsletter

COST: N60,000 annual

Gold Membership

Benefits include:

  • Everything in Silver Membership
  • Priority spotlight position on the home page
  • Speaking slots at chamber events
  • Free stand space at the annual business expo
  • Direct introductions to chamber partners

COST: N120,000 annual
